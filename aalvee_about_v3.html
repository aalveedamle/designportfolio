Hello!

I'm a Senior Product Designer shaping evidence-driven experiences at the intersection of AI, banking, risk, and innovation.

My design journey started with a paintbrush — and it continues to fascinate me. Every project is a new puzzle: human, technical, business, all at once. This page is the long answer to "tell me about yourself."

From painting as a child to strategizing design solutions for global brands, my journey has always been fueled by creativity. I see design as more than aesthetics — it's a tool to solve problems and improve lives.

During my master's program, I became fascinated with how the human mind works, realizing that my creative skills could be harnessed to make real-world impact.

At EY, I had the chance to lead design and research for AI-powered tools, regulated banking platforms, and future-state innovation engagements — proving the power of design to drive business outcomes alongside user trust.

Growing up in India, where innovation often means doing more with less, I learned to approach challenges with simplicity and resourcefulness — a "jugaad" mindset that shapes every system I design today, where I strive for solutions that are not only effective but also intuitive and scalable.

My Approach

Creativity, grounded in evidence.

I blend creativity with strategy to create impactful, user-centered solutions. My process starts with deep research to understand user needs, followed by strategic problem-solving that aligns design with business goals.

I prioritize simplicity and usability, ensuring solutions are intuitive and easy to navigate. Collaboration is core — I work closely with cross-functional teams to bring ideas to life, with a focus on innovation and sustainability so designs are effective today and future-proof tomorrow.

I measure success through metrics, refining designs to continually improve user experience and business outcomes.
