Featured Case Studies

Case Study 01

An AI Co-pilot Auditors Actually Trust

Reframing a black-box GenAI tool inside a Big Four risk platform — designing for transparency, recoverability, and the auditor's mental model of evidence.

Problem Auditors couldn't trust AI-generated evidence — no transparency, no control.
Solution Redesigned the GenAI layer with evidence trails, confidence indicators, and override flows.
Outcome Adopted across EY risk teams; GenAI reframed from liability to trusted co-pilot.
GenAI · Risk Trust & Transparency
Case Study 02

Embedding AI Into the Workflow, Not Beside It

A contextual AI widget built to live inside EY's risk platform — bringing AI to the exact moment auditors need it, without breaking their workflow or trust.

Problem Auditors valued GenIAus but resented leaving their risk dashboard to use a separate AI tool.
Solution Designed Genie — a layered contextual widget embedded in the VIA platform with page awareness and deep workflow integration.
Outcome −60% context-switching; time-to-first-query dropped from 4 min to ~45s; +28% trust rating.
GenAI · EY Embedded AI
Case Study 03

Empowering Farmers Through Sustainable Practice

A research-led AgriTech tool for the World Bank that became EY's first engagement considered for patent application — translating field research into a mobile-first system.

Problem Smallholder farmers had no accessible tool to adopt sustainable practices at scale.
Solution Field-research-led mobile tool built around real farmer workflows — simple, vernacular-ready.
Outcome EY's first patent-considered engagement; validated by the World Bank as a replicable model.
World Bank · AgriTech Patent-pending
